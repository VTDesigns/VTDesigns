Idea Studio

You have a picture in your head.
Let's build it.

Describe the picture you want to make. The Idea Studio turns it into a real drawing project, the way I teach in my studio: where your eyes are, which perspective to use, how to arrange it, and how to build it step by step from simple forms to the finished piece. Then take it to paint in oil, acrylic or watercolor, with a palette and a painting process for your picture.

"Construction before detail. Get the eye level right and the rest follows."
