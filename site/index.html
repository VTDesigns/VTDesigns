the work itself

Every lesson is built around an original painting by Vaughn Tucker — a working fine artist with decades of experience in oil, acrylic, watercolor, and pencil. You don't watch someone describe art. You step inside a real painting and learn how it was made, stroke by stroke.

The interactive canvas lets you toggle perspective guides on and off, drag vanishing points, sketch directly over the artwork, and save your progress. It's not passive. It never was.

Your Teacher

Vaughn Tucker —
Fine Artist

Born in St. Ann, Jamaica, Vaughn Tucker is a self-taught fine artist whose work spans oil, acrylic, watercolor, and pencil. His paintings capture Caribbean light, community, and the beauty of the everyday — scenes that draw you in and teach you to see differently.

His YouTube channel, Vaughn Tucker Art, brings his teaching to a global audience. This academy is the next step — an interactive experience where his original artwork becomes your classroom.
