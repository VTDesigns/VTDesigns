For parents, teachers and homeschools

A safe, simple place to learn art

Children can start drawing right away. Here is what you should know.

Ask about using it with your class

  • The drawing and painting tools are freeNo account and no password. Drawings stay on your own device.
  • Children under 13 need a parent's emailIf your child wants to join the email list, please sign up with your own email address.
  • Sharing is up to youChildren should ask a grown-up before posting a picture to the student gallery or making a video to share.
  • Lessons are free and paidMany lessons are free. Paid courses and critiques are always clearly marked, with prices shown before checkout.
  • Homeschools and schoolsUsing the Academy with a group of students? Send a message and Vaughn will help you set it up.

Your teacher

Vaughn Tucker

Born in St. Ann, Jamaica, Vaughn Tucker is a self-taught fine artist who paints in oil, acrylic, watercolour and pencil. His paintings capture Caribbean light, community and the beauty of everyday places.

He teaches art at school and on his YouTube channel, Vaughn Tucker Art. This Academy is where his own paintings become your classroom.
